# Athlete and Sports Participation: Gender Parity & Discipline Trajectories

**Project:** Global Olympic Sports Performance Analysis  
**Author:** Md. Firdous Alam  
**Live Dashboard:** [Tableau Public Profile](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/md.firdous.alam/viz/GlobalOlympicSportsPerformanceAnalysis_17933559199720/Dashboard3)  

## Overview
Investigation into athletic demographics, tracking female participation from 2.2% in 1900 to 45.0% in 2016.

## Key Analytical Highlights
- Female representation rose from 2.2% (Paris 1900) to 45.0% (Rio 2016)
- Accelerated female growth post-1972 Title IX and IOC mandatory inclusion charters
- Discipline expansion: Number of sports expanded from 9 in 1896 to 34 in 2016
