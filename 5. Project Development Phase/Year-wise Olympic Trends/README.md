# Year-wise Olympic Trends: Centennial Evolution of Games & Participation

**Project:** Global Olympic Sports Performance Analysis  
**Author:** Md. Firdous Alam  
**Live Dashboard:** [Tableau Public Profile](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/md.firdous.alam/viz/GlobalOlympicSportsPerformanceAnalysis_17933559199720/Dashboard3)  

## Overview
Longitudinal analysis of Olympic expansion from 1896 Athens to 2016 Rio, examining participation growth and geopolitical disruption.

## Key Analytical Highlights
- Growth in athlete entries: 241 athletes in 1896 to 11,179 in Rio 2016
- Geopolitical disruptions: Cancelled Games (1916, 1940, 1944) and boycotts (1980 Moscow, 1984 Los Angeles)
- Expansion of nations: 14 nations in 1896 to 207 NOCs in 2016
